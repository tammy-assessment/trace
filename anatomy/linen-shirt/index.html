Where the leftover goes

$50.29 to the shop, $16.33 to the brand and $5.44 to the factory owner - none of it the wage.

No retailer publishes its margin, so this is not their books. It is Clean Clothes Campaign’s decomposition of a €29 t-shirt - 59 points to the shop, 12 to the brand, 4 to the factory owner - applied as a ratio to the remainder left after this item’s own materials, freight and wage.

The shop and the brand together keep 38× what the person who made it took home.

Linen biodegrades. Polyester thread, interfacing and labels do not.

Australia landfilled 222,000 tonnes of textiles in 2023 (The Australia Institute, 2024).

linen decomposes anaerobically into methane; the polyester thread that outlasts it fragments instead

the linen goes; the polyester thread, interfacing, labels and buttons are roughly a twentieth of the weight and stay

The layers are depth, the scale on the right is time. The object does not travel between them - it stays where it was tipped, and the years pass around it.
